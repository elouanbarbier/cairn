# Code of conduct

Cairn is built by volunteers, for people who use it. Everyone taking part in its issues, pull requests and
discussions is expected to make that a good experience for others.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a language that is not their own.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Disagreement is fine; contempt is not.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change that does not fit the project's scope.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background or abilities.
- Sexualized language or imagery, threats, and personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Repeatedly pushing a topic after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If something here is not respected, contact the maintainers privately through the email address shown on the
maintainers' GitHub profiles. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, and block people who do not follow this code, in
proportion to what happened. This code applies in all of the project's spaces, and when someone represents the
project elsewhere.
